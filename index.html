Our Story

"Spanish and mediterranean dishes inspired by tradition"

El Rincón means “the hidden corner”, a place where people come together to enjoy good food, warmth, and relaxed Mediterranean hospitality.

We are a family-run Spanish and Mediterranean restaurant in Sunningdale, near Ascot, serving authentic dishes inspired by Spain and the wider Mediterranean.

Our food is rooted in tradition, driven by flavour, and made using quality ingredients and time-honoured techniques. From classic Spanish favourites to Mediterranean staples, every dish reflects care, consistency, and a genuine love of cooking.
